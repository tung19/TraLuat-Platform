# Vietnam's án lệ (Precedent) System and Official Court-Judgment Publication Infrastructure

Research current as of early November 2026. Several items (exact live counts; whether a given resolution is still in force verbatim) could only be verified to mid-2026 or from secondary sources; these are flagged in Gaps. Primary resolution texts live on thuvienphapluat.vn and vbpl.vn, which blocked automated fetching during this research, so some article-level detail comes from reputable secondary summaries (law journals, law firms, lawnet.vn English).

## Legal framework for án lệ: governing Resolutions, process, and whether courts must apply them

### Takeaway
Vietnam's precedent regime was created by Resolution 03/2015/NQ-HĐTP (first ever) and is now governed by **Resolution 04/2019/NQ-HĐTP** (issued 18 June 2019, effective 15 July 2019), which replaced 03/2015. Resolution 04/2019 is still the base instrument; it was amended (not replaced) by **Resolution 02/2025/NQ-HĐTP** effective 1 July 2025, and a consolidated text exists (11/VBHN-TANDTC). Courts have a qualified duty to apply precedent: they must apply a relevant precedent or expressly justify in the judgment why they did not. "Nghị quyết 04/2025/NQ-HĐTP" is a DIFFERENT, unrelated instrument (about the criminal-law aggravating factor "phạm tội có tính chất côn đồ") — not the precedent-process resolution; do not confuse the two.

### Cited Findings
- Resolution 04/2019/NQ-HĐTP governs how án lệ are selected, published, and applied; issued 18 June 2019, effective 15 July 2019, replacing Resolution 03/2015 (the first-ever precedent resolution in Vietnam's court system) — [Lawnet summary](https://lawnet.vn/thong-tin-phap-luat/en/an-le/what-is-the-precedent-criteria-for-selection-and-ratification-of-precedents-in-vietnam-113244.html); [dulieuphapluat.vn copy of Resolution 04/2019](https://dulieuphapluat.vn/van-ban/to-tung-van-ban/resolution-042019nq-hdtp-on-the-process-of-selecting-announcing-and-applying-case-precedent-issued-by-the-council-of-judges-of-the-supreme-people39s-court-1009907.html)
- Resolution 02/2025/NQ-HĐTP (effective 1 July 2025) amended several HĐTP resolutions including 04/2019 — e.g., it amended point c, clause 2, Article 6 to add proposals from the Judicial Committee of provincial-level People's Courts (Ủy ban Thẩm phán TAND cấp tỉnh) as a route to precedent, reflecting the 2024/2025 court reorganization; some appended forms of 04/2019 were replaced — [Tạp chí Tòa án](https://tapchitoaan.vn/index.php/tu-ngay-0172025-nghi-quyet-so-022025nq-hdtp-chinh-thuc-co-hieu-luc-thi-hanh-bao-dam-ap-dung-dung-va-thong-nhat-mot-so-quy-dinh-cua-luat-pha-san-bo-luat-to-tung-dan-su-luat-to-tung-hanh-chinh-bo-luat-hinh-su13661.html)
- A consolidated document (văn bản hợp nhất) No. 11/VBHN-TANDTC keeps Resolution 04/2019 intact and folds in the 02/2025 amendments — [lsvn.vn "Mấy vấn đề về án lệ ở Việt Nam"](https://lsvn.vn/may-van-de-ve-an-le-o-viet-nam-a171442.html)
- Recognition of precedent also now rests on the 2024 Law on Organization of People's Courts together with Resolution 04/2019 — [vietnamlawmagazine.vn](https://vietnamlawmagazine.vn/civil-precedents-in-vietnam-legal-grounds-and-practice-69872.html)
- Four routes by which a judgment/decision reaches the Council of Judges for adoption as precedent: (a) a legally effective judgment/decision that has gone through consultation under Articles 4–5 of Resolution 04/2019; (b) proposal by a Supreme People's Court Judge; (c) proposal by a High People's Court Judicial Committee; (d) selection by the Council of Judges during cassation (giám đốc thẩm) or reopening (tái thẩm) review — [vnlawfirm.vn](https://vnlawfirm.vn/what-is-a-precedent-regulations-on-adoption-and-promulgation-of-precedents)
- Adoption: the Chief Justice (Chánh án TANDTC) convenes a plenary session of the Council of Judges (Hội đồng Thẩm phán); at least two-thirds of total members must attend and a majority vote is required; results are recorded in the session minutes as the basis for promulgation — [vnlawfirm.vn](https://vnlawfirm.vn/what-is-a-precedent-regulations-on-adoption-and-promulgation-of-precedents)
- Process also involves a public-comment window and an advisory council: comments collected for ~30 days from posting, and the Chief Justice forms an án lệ advisory council (Hội đồng tư vấn án lệ) of at least 9 members (secondary summary of Resolution 04/2019 process) — [WebSearch synthesis of Resolution 04/2019; luatminhkhue process article (blocked on fetch)](https://luatminhkhue.vn/quy-trinh-lua-chon-cong-bo-va-ap-dung-an-le-theo-nghi-quyet-04-2019-nq-hdtp.aspx)
- Promulgation: the Chief Justice issues a decision (Quyết định ... /QĐ-CA); the precedent is posted on the Supreme People's Court portal, sent to all courts/units, and included in the official Collection of Precedents (Tuyển tập án lệ) — [vnlawfirm.vn](https://vnlawfirm.vn/what-is-a-precedent-regulations-on-adoption-and-promulgation-of-precedents)
- Application timing: a precedent is studied and applied in adjudication 30 days after promulgation — [vnlawfirm.vn](https://vnlawfirm.vn/what-is-a-precedent-regulations-on-adoption-and-promulgation-of-precedents); one secondary source instead states 45 days or the date in the Chief Justice's decision — [tapchitoaan.vn comparative article](https://tapchitoaan.vn/%C2%A0cong-bo-va-pho-bien-an-le-o-viet-nam-%C2%A0so-sanh-voi-australia-phap-nhat-ban-va-trung-quoc10688.html)
- Obligation to apply: judges and lay assessors MUST study and apply precedents to ensure consistent rulings in cases with similar legal circumstances; if a court does NOT apply an on-point precedent, it must clearly state its reasons in the judgment/decision; when applying one, the court must cite and analyze the precedent's number, name, legal situation and solution in the "Court's Reasoning" (Nhận định của Tòa án) section — [Lawnet](https://lawnet.vn/thong-tin-phap-luat/en/an-le/what-is-the-precedent-criteria-for-selection-and-ratification-of-precedents-in-vietnam-113244.html)
- Annulment/superseding (Article 9 of Resolution 04/2019): a precedent is (i) automatically annulled (đương nhiên bãi bỏ) when it no longer fits because the law has changed, or (ii) annulled by decision of the Council of Judges; Article 10 sets the procedure for proposing annulment. Example: Án lệ 08/2016/AL was automatically annulled after Resolution 01/2019/NQ-HĐTP provided a governing legal rule — [luatminhkhue](https://luatminhkhue.vn/an-le-da-cong-bo-bi-bai-bo-trong-nhung-truong-hop-nao.aspx); [tapchitoaan.vn on Án lệ 08/2016/AL](https://tapchitoaan.vn/an-le-08-2016-al-duong-nhien-bi-bai-bo)
- Where a court has quashed/modified the underlying judgment of a precedent (point b, clause 2, Article 9), it must report to TANDTC within 5 working days for consideration of annulment — [lsvn.vn](https://lsvn.vn/an-le-bi-bai-bo-trong-truong-hop-nao-1680492407-a129101.html)
- "Nghị quyết 04/2025/NQ-HĐTP" (dated ~30 Sept 2025) concerns the criminal-law factor "phạm tội có tính chất côn đồ," NOT the precedent process — [tapchitoaan.vn](https://tapchitoaan.vn/thong-qua-nghi-quyet-huong-dan-ap-dung-mot-so-quy-dinh-cua-bo-luat-dan-su-ve-trach-nhiem-boi-thuong-thiet-hai-ngoai-hop-dong7085.html)

### Inferences
- Vietnam's án lefficacy is "soft/persuasive-mandatory": precedent is not stare decisis as in common law, but the mandatory-cite-or-justify rule gives it real operative force. A legal-AI tool evaluating whether a judgment is "correct" should treat an on-point, in-force precedent as something the court was required to either follow or distinguish with stated reasons — the absence of such reasoning is itself a defect signal.
- The 2024 Law on Organization of People's Courts plus the 2025 reorganization (addition of provincial Judicial Committee proposals) means the institutional pipeline changed in 2025; a product should track the consolidated text (11/VBHN-TANDTC), not the raw 2019 text alone.

### Gaps
- Exact step counts conflict across secondary sources (one says 4 steps, another 6); the precise article-by-article text of Articles 4–10 of Resolution 04/2019 was not fetched from a primary source (thuvienphapluat.vn/vbpl.vn blocked). Recommend verifying against the official consolidated text 11/VBHN-TANDTC.
- The 30-day vs 45-day application-timing discrepancy is unresolved; Resolution 04/2019 as commonly summarized says 30 days — verify in the primary text.

## Structure of a published án lệ and how it ties to specific điều luật

### Takeaway
A published án lệ is a structured document: number+name, the source judgment/decision it derives from, keywords, the legal situation (tình huống án lệ), the legal solution (giải pháp pháp lý), the cited statutory provisions it interprets (các quy định của pháp luật liên quan), the case summary/facts, and the precedent "content" (nội dung án lệ) quoted from the Court's Reasoning. The tie to statute is explicit: each án lệ lists the specific điều/khoản it interprets or fills.

### Cited Findings
- A promulgated precedent includes: its number and name; the number/name of the source judgment or decision; the legal situation and the solution; the relevant legal provisions; keywords on situation and solution; case details and relevant court rulings; and the precedent's content — [Lawnet (summarizing Resolution 04/2019 structure)](https://lawnet.vn/thong-tin-phap-luat/en/an-le/what-is-the-precedent-criteria-for-selection-and-ratification-of-precedents-in-vietnam-113244.html)
- Selection criteria (Article 1, Resolution 04/2019): the precedent must (a) clarify legal provisions open to differing interpretations OR identify applicable principles for facts not explicitly regulated by law; (b) exemplify legal standards/reasoning; (c) guide the uniform application of law in adjudication — [vnlawfirm.vn](https://vnlawfirm.vn/what-is-a-precedent-regulations-on-adoption-and-promulgation-of-precedents)
- When applying a precedent, the court must cite its number, name, the legal situation and the legal solution, and analyze them in the Court's Reasoning section — showing that án lệ operate at the level of a (situation → solution) rule bound to specific statutory provisions — [Lawnet](https://lawnet.vn/thong-tin-phap-luat/en/an-le/what-is-the-precedent-criteria-for-selection-and-ratification-of-precedents-in-vietnam-113244.html)

### Inferences
- The canonical published án lệ layout (as seen on anle.toaan.gov.vn) is: Nguồn án lệ (source) → Vị trí nội dung án lệ (location in the source judgment, e.g., specific paragraph) → Khái quát nội dung án lệ → Quy định của pháp luật liên quan đến án lệ (the điều luật interpreted) → Từ khóa án lệ → Nội dung vụ án → Nội dung án lệ. For a legal-AI data model, the two load-bearing fields are "tình huống án lệ" (operative fact pattern) and "giải pháp pháp lý" (the holding), each linked to the enumerated statutory provisions — this is the natural join key between a precedent and the statute graph.
- Because each án lệ names the exact provisions it interprets, a product can index precedents against điều/khoản and surface the correct precedent when reasoning about a judgment that turns on those provisions — but must respect effective-dating (below), since the interpreted provision may since have been amended (which can auto-annul the precedent).

### Gaps
- The precise field labels/ordering were inferred from the Resolution's enumerated content plus known portal layout, not quoted from a freshly fetched anle.toaan.gov.vn page (homepage not retrievable via search). Recommend confirming field names against a live án lệ page.

## Official sources and access terms (án lệ portal; judgment-publication portal; reuse/scraping/anonymization)

### Takeaway
Two official TANDTC portals: **anle.toaan.gov.vn** (precedents) and **congbobanan.toaan.gov.vn** (the "Cổng công bố bản án, quyết định của Tòa án" for ordinary judgments). Judgment publication is governed by **Resolution 03/2017/NQ-HĐTP** (effective 1 July 2017) plus implementing Official Letter 144/TANDTC-PC (4 July 2017). Judgments are anonymized by letter-coding before publication. No public API or documented bulk-access/reuse terms were found for congbobanan. Separately, Vietnamese IP law (Article 15, Law on Intellectual Property) excludes legal, administrative and "other judicial-field documents" and their official translations from copyright — a strong argument that judgment/precedent TEXT is not copyright-protected, though portal access rules and personal-data law still constrain use.

### Cited Findings
- Judgment publication governed by Resolution 03/2017/NQ-HĐTP (adopted 16 March 2017, effective 1 July 2017); portal is congbobanan.toaan.gov.vn, launched July 2017; implementing guidance is Official Letter 144/TANDTC-PC dated 4 July 2017 — [vietnam-business-law blog](https://vietnam-business-law.squarespace.com/blog/2017/7/17/online-publication-of-courts-judgments-in-vietnam); [dulieuphapluat.vn copy of Resolution 03/2017](https://dulieuphapluat.vn/van-ban/buu-chinh-vien-thong/nghi-quyet-032017nq-hdtp-cong-bo-ban-an-quyet-dinh-tren-cong-thong-tin-dien-tu-cua-toa-an-do-hoi-dong-tham-phan-toa-an-nhan-dan-toi-cao-ban-hanh-108006.html)
- What must be published: legally effective judgments/decisions of the people's courts, from 1 July 2017 onward; must be posted within 30 days of taking effect; Official Letter 144 limits the mandatory requirement to judgments issued from 1 July 2017 (earlier ones not required) — [vietnam-business-law blog](https://vietnam-business-law.squarespace.com/blog/2017/7/17/online-publication-of-courts-judgments-in-vietnam)
- Exclusions from publication (Article 4 of Resolution 03/2017): closed-session (xét xử kín) cases; state-secret / national-security content; undisclosed business know-how/technology where confidentiality was requested; content harmful to recognized cultural traditions; cases involving a party under 18; unencrypted personal/family secrets; and cases where a participant validly requested confidentiality — [vietnam-business-law blog](https://vietnam-business-law.squarespace.com/blog/2017/7/17/online-publication-of-courts-judgments-in-vietnam); [tapchitoaan.vn](https://tapchitoaan.vn/index.php/vuong-mac-trong-viec-thuc-hien-quy-dinh-ve-viec-cong-bo-ban-an-quyet-dinh-tren-cong-thong-tin-dien-tu-cua-toa-an)
- Anonymization: before publication the court must encrypt/code information constituting personal and family secrets (clause 2, Article 7, Resolution 03/2017); per Official Letter 144, coding uses Vietnamese-alphabet letters, optionally combined with natural numbers (e.g., a defendant's full name replaced by initials/letters) — [tapchitoaan.vn (via WebSearch synthesis of Official Letter 144)](https://tapchitoaan.vn/index.php/vuong-mac-trong-viec-thuc-hien-quy-dinh-ve-viec-cong-bo-ban-an-quyet-dinh-tren-cong-thong-tin-dien-tu-cua-toa-an); [hoatieu.vn copy of Official Letter 144](https://hoatieu.vn/phap-luat/cong-van-144-tandtc-pc-nam-2017-thi-hanh-nghi-quyet-03-2017-nq-hdtp-127084)
- Parties' rights: the presiding judge must inform parties of publication and of their right to request non-publication of personal/family/trade secrets — [vietnam-business-law blog](https://vietnam-business-law.squarespace.com/blog/2017/7/17/online-publication-of-courts-judgments-in-vietnam)
- Án lệ portal: precedents are posted on TANDTC's electronic án lệ portal, sent to all courts/units, and compiled into the Tuyển tập án lệ, reportedly published periodically (~every 12 months) — [tapchitoaan.vn comparative article](https://tapchitoaan.vn/%C2%A0cong-bo-va-pho-bien-an-le-o-viet-nam-%C2%A0so-sanh-voi-australia-phap-nhat-ban-va-trung-quoc10688.html)
- Copyright exclusion: Article 15 of the Law on Intellectual Property lists objects NOT protected by copyright — clause 1 pure current-news; clause 2 "Văn bản quy phạm pháp luật, văn bản hành chính, văn bản khác thuộc lĩnh vực tư pháp" and their official translations; clause 3 processes/systems/methods/concepts/principles/data — [hethongphapluat.com, Law on IP 2005, Article 15](https://hethongphapluat.com/luat-so-huu-tri-tue-2005/dieu-15)

### Inferences
- Court judgments and án lệ plausibly fall within Article 15(2)'s "văn bản khác thuộc lĩnh vực tư pháp" (other judicial-field documents), meaning the TEXT itself is not subject to copyright. This supports legitimate ingestion/reuse of the substantive legal content by a commercial product. HOWEVER this is an interpretive point (Article 15 does not say "bản án" verbatim) and does NOT override: (i) the portal's own access/terms and anti-abuse controls; (ii) personal-data/privacy obligations (published judgments are already anonymized, but re-identification or scraping of personal data could raise issues under Vietnam's Personal Data Protection law); (iii) TANDTC's authority over its systems.
- No public API, bulk-download endpoint, or published reuse license was found for congbobanan.toaan.gov.vn. Practically, access is via the web portal's search UI ("Bản Án - Quyết Định"). A compliant product should seek an official data-sharing arrangement with TANDTC's IT department rather than assume scraping is permitted — Vietnamese government data-sharing generally runs through authenticated extraction accounts issued by the owning agency's IT department (the pattern documented for the Ministry of Justice's VBPL legal database). Treat congbobanan as "public to read, no documented bulk/API right" pending official confirmation.
- Per the research constraint, commercial aggregators (thuvienphapluat.vn, luatvietnam.vn, lawnet.vn) should NOT be scraped; they are cited here only as secondary descriptive sources.

### Gaps
- Could not find any official terms-of-use, robots policy, API documentation, or bulk-access/licensing statement for congbobanan.toaan.gov.vn or anle.toaan.gov.vn. Whether scraping is technically restricted or officially permitted is unverified — must be confirmed with TANDTC directly.
- Total number of judgments published on congbobanan (scale of the corpus) was not found in authoritative figures.
- Whether "bản án" is definitively inside Article 15(2) vs. merely analogous is not settled by a cited authority; recommend a formal IP-law opinion. Note Article 15 was reportedly amended in 2025 — verify the current consolidated wording.

## Effective-dating: applicable law is the law in force at the relevant time

### Takeaway
Vietnamese doctrine confirms the core principle: a legal act/behavior is judged under the law in force at the time it occurred (temporal effect, hiệu lực theo thời gian), even if that law has since lapsed; retroactivity is the exception and generally allowed only when a later rule is more favorable (notably in administrative-sanction and criminal law). This is codified in the Law on Promulgation of Legislative Documents (Article 156 of the 2015 Law; now the 2025 Law No. 64/2025/QH15, Articles 55 and 58).

### Cited Findings
- General adjudication principle: the legality of conduct that occurred long ago must be assessed under the law in force at the time of the conduct, even if that law is no longer effective — [tapchitoaan.vn "Nguyên tắc chung về áp dụng pháp luật khi xét xử"](https://tapchitoaan.vn/index.php/nguyen-tac-chung-ve-ap-dung-phap-luat-khi-xet-xu)
- A document generally applies to conduct occurring while it is in effect; exception: if a new document imposes no liability or a lighter liability, the new (more favorable) rule applies to pre-effective conduct — based on Article 156 of the 2015 Law on Promulgation of Legislative Documents — [lsvn.vn](https://lsvn.vn/nguyen-tac-ap-dung-phap-luat-trong-xu-ly-vi-pham-hanh-chinh-a47002.html)
- Criminal law: a law applies only to offenses committed while it is in force (non-retroactivity, bất hồi tố, subject to the more-favorable-to-defendant exception) — [luatminhkhue.vn on bất hồi tố](https://luatminhkhue.vn/bat-hoi-to-la-gi---khai-niem-ve-bat-hoi-to--.aspx)
- Current statute: the 2025 Law on Promulgation of Legislative Documents (Law No. 64/2025/QH15, 19 Feb 2025) governs temporal effect (Article 58) and retroactivity (Article 55), replacing the 2015 Law's Article 156 — [hethongphapluat.com, Law 64/2025/QH15, Article 58](https://hethongphapluat.com/law-no-64-2025-qh15-dated-february-19-2025-on-promulgation-of-legislative-documents/dieu-58); [thuvienphapluat.vn](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/hoi-dap-phap-luat/van-ban-quy-pham-phap-luat-co-hieu-luc-khi-nao-138052731.html)

### Inferences
- For a product assessing whether a past judgment is "correct," the controlling legal baseline must be the statute text as in force at the operative time (date of the conduct / transaction / dispute), not the current text — and likewise the applicable án lệ are those in force (published and not yet annulled) at that time. This requires versioned/effective-dated statute AND precedent data: the engine must resolve, for any given date, (1) which statutory wording applied, and (2) which án lệ were in force, because an án lệ is automatically annulled when its underlying provision changes (Article 9(1), Resolution 04/2019). The narrow favorable-retroactivity exception (lighter/eliminated liability in sanctions and criminal law) is the main carve-out to encode.

### Gaps
- The full text of Articles 55 and 58 of Law 64/2025/QH15 was not fetched in detail; verify the exact retroactivity conditions and any transitional rules against the primary text.
