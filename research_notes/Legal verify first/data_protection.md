# Vietnamese Personal Data Protection Legal Framework (verification for a legal-AI SaaS handling client data)

_Current as of 2026-11-05. Each claim is marked VERIFIED (with source) or UNVERIFIED/CONFLICTING. Primary-text article numbers are given where found._

## Claim 1: Decree 13/2023/ND-CP (PDPD) existed and was effective 1 July 2023

### Takeaway
VERIFIED. Decree 13/2023/ND-CP on Personal Data Protection existed, was dated 17 April 2023, and took effect 1 July 2023. It is no longer the current primary instrument (see Claim 2).

### Cited Findings
- Decree No. 13/2023/ND-CP is dated April 17, 2023 ("Decree No. 13/2023/ND-CP dated April 17, 2023 of the Government on personal data protection") — [LuatVietnam English index](https://english.luatvietnam.vn/decree-no-13-2023-nd-cp-dated-april-17-2023-of-the-government-on-personal-data-protection-249791-doc1.html)
- Effective date 1 July 2023, with no transitional/grace period — multiple firm alerts (CMS, Allen & Gledhill, WFW, Tilleke, KPMG) — [CMS Law](https://cms.law/en/int/legal-updates/vietnam-law-on-personal-data-protection-to-take-effect-from-1-july-2023); [Allen & Gledhill](https://www.allenandgledhill.com/perspectives/articles/23649/vnkh-personal-data-protection-decree-in-effect-from-1-july-2023); [Lawnet Q&A confirming 1 July 2023](https://lawnet.vn/en/laws/does-the-decree-13-2023-nd-cp-on-protection-of-personal-data-in-vietnam-come-into-effect-from-july-99565.html)
- Decree 13 was Vietnam's first comprehensive data-protection instrument (a Decree, not a Law) — [Tilleke](https://www.tilleke.com/insights/vietnam-issues-landmark-personal-data-protection-decree/); [KPMG](https://kpmg.com/vn/en/home/insights/2023/04/legal-alert-on-decree-13.html)

### Inferences
- The task prompt's label "PDPD" for Decree 13 matches common firm shorthand (Personal Data Protection Decree). Correct.

### Gaps
- None material. Note the prompt's parenthetical is slightly off: Decree 13 was a Decree, not a "Law"; the Law came in 2025 (Claim 2).

## Claim 2: Decree 13 is SUPERSEDED by Decree 356/2025/ND-CP, effective 1 January 2026, as the guiding decree under a new Law on Personal Data Protection

### Takeaway
VERIFIED, with one correction to terminology. The new primary instrument is a **Law** (Law No. 91/2025/QH15 on Personal Data Protection), passed 26 June 2025, effective **1 January 2026**. Its guiding decree is **Decree No. 356/2025/ND-CP, dated 31 December 2025, effective 1 January 2026**, which replaces Decree 13/2023/ND-CP. So the chain is: Law 91/2025/QH15 (primary) + Decree 356/2025/ND-CP (guiding decree) → Decree 13/2023/ND-CP ceases effect 1 Jan 2026.

### Cited Findings
- **Law number and dates (PRIMARY TEXT):** "Law on Personal Data Protection, No. 91/2025/QH15"; passed 26 June 2025 by the 15th National Assembly (9th session), signed by NA Chairman Tran Thanh Man; **Article 38(1): "This Law takes effect on January 1, 2026."** Structure: 5 chapters, 39 articles — [LuatVietnam English text of Law 91/2025/QH15](https://english.luatvietnam.vn/dan-su/law-on-personal-data-protection-law-no-91-2025-qh15-405135-d1.html)
- Passage date 26 June 2025 and effective 1 Jan 2026 also confirmed by — [Baker McKenzie](https://connectontech.bakermckenzie.com/vietnams-pdpl-in-focus-what-to-know-and-watch-for/)
- **Decree 356/2025/ND-CP dated 31 December 2025**, "detailing a number of articles and measures for the implementation of the Law on Personal Data Protection" — [LuatVietnam English index](https://english.luatvietnam.vn/decree-no-356-2025-nd-cp-dated-december-31-2025-of-the-government-detailing-a-number-of-articles-and-measures-for-the-implementation-of-the-law-on-p-422896-doc1.html)
- Decree 356 "takes effect on January 1, 2026 and replaces Decree 13/2023/ND-CP" — [LuatVietnam legal update: new features of Decree 356 vs Decree 13](https://luatvietnam.vn/legal-updates/new-features-of-decree-356-2025-compared-with-decree-13-2023-on-personal-data-protection-892-106712-article.html)
- Decree 356 took effect 1 Jan 2026 concurrently with the Law, superseding Decree 13 — [EY Vietnam legal alert, March 2026](https://www.ey.com/en_vn/technical/tax/tax-and-law-updates/legal-alert-march-2026-decree-no-356-2025-nd-cp-providing-detailed-guidance-for-implementation-of-personal-data-protection-law); [PwC Vietnam legal brief 28 Jan 2026](https://www.pwc.com/vn/en/publications/legal-news-brief/20260128-new-rules-personal-data-protection.html); [Rajah & Tann](https://www.rajahtannasia.com/viewpoints/new-decree-implementing-the-law-on-personal-data-protection-passed/)
- Decree 356 structure: reported as 5 chapters / 42 articles by firm summaries (vs Law's 39 articles) — [Mondaq / Rouse summary](https://rouse.com/insights/news/2026/vietnam-key-developments-in-personal-data-protection-under-decree-no-356-2025-nd-cp-and-issues-businesses-need-to-review)
- **Transition:** Law Article 39(2) and Decree 356 allow impact-assessment dossiers already received by the Ministry of Public Security under Decree 13 to continue in use; later updates governed by the new Law — [LuatVietnam law text](https://english.luatvietnam.vn/dan-su/law-on-personal-data-protection-law-no-91-2025-qh15-405135-d1.html); [Baker McKenzie](https://connectontech.bakermckenzie.com/vietnams-pdpl-in-focus-what-to-know-and-watch-for/)

### Inferences
- The prompt's framing ("Decree 356 supersedes Decree 13 as the guiding decree under a new Law") is correct, but note the new primary law (91/2025/QH15), not the decree, is the top of the hierarchy. For a SaaS compliance posture, cite the Law for substantive obligations and Decree 356 for operational detail/templates.

### Gaps / Cautions
- CONFLICT (resolved): Vietnam Briefing's July 2025 commentary is internally inconsistent (says both 1 Jan 2026 and 1 July 2026). The PRIMARY law text (Article 38(1)) says **1 January 2026** — treat 1 July 2026 as an error. — [Vietnam Briefing](https://www.vietnam-briefing.com/news/vietnam-law-on-personal-data-protection-latest-developments-and-insights.html/)
- A separate **administrative sanctioning decree** ("Sanctioning Decree 2.0") setting concrete fine amounts was still in draft, expected first half of 2026 — status unconfirmed as of these sources. — [LuatVietnam / Rouse](https://luatvietnam.vn/legal-updates/new-features-of-decree-356-2025-compared-with-decree-13-2023-on-personal-data-protection-892-106712-article.html)

## Claim 3: Under 356/2025, cross-border transfer is defined broadly to INCLUDE cloud-based processing, with a mandatory Transfer Impact Assessment (TIA) dossier, and MPS can halt transfers

### Takeaway
LARGELY VERIFIED. The broad cross-border definition and the TIA/dossier obligation sit primarily in **Law 91/2025/QH15 Article 20** (Decree 356 supplies procedures, dossiers, order, and 10 templates, with cloud computing addressed at Decree 356 Article 12). The definition expressly reaches processing-on-overseas-platforms/cloud/overseas servers. MPS (Ministry of Public Security, A05 unit) receives/appraises the TIA and can prohibit/suspend transfers; the exact statutory wording of the suspension power is only in secondary sources.

### Cited Findings — Cross-border definition (incl. cloud)
- **PRIMARY TEXT — Law Article 20(1)** lists three cases counting as cross-border transfer: (a) moving personal data stored in Vietnam to a storage system outside Vietnam; (b) a Vietnamese agency/organization/individual transferring personal data to overseas organizations/individuals; (c) **platforms located outside Vietnam processing personal data collected in Vietnam** — [LuatVietnam law text](https://english.luatvietnam.vn/dan-su/law-on-personal-data-protection-law-no-91-2025-qh15-405135-d1.html)
- Baker McKenzie: PDPL replaced the narrower "overseas transfer" with a sweeping definition covering exporting data stored in Vietnam, domestic-to-foreign transfers, and **processing local collections on external platforms** — [Baker McKenzie](https://connectontech.bakermckenzie.com/vietnams-pdpl-in-focus-what-to-know-and-watch-for/)
- EY: enterprises are "still deemed to have conducted a cross-border transfer" when **using cloud or a data server located overseas** to store or process data — [EY July 2025 alert](https://www.ey.com/en_vn/technical/tax/tax-and-law-updates/legal-alert-july-2025-personal-data-protection-law)
- Digital Policy Alert (secondary) attributes to the implementing decree a definition covering transfer of data collected/stored in Vietnam to server systems outside Vietnam **or cloud computing services of foreign providers** — [Digital Policy Alert](https://digitalpolicyalert.org/event/36569-implementing-regulations-of-law-on-personal-data-protection-including-cross-border-data-transfer-regulation-entered-into-force)
- Decree 356 addresses **"Cloud computing" at Article 12** (per LuatVietnam feature comparison; the page gives the article heading but not full text) — [LuatVietnam feature comparison](https://luatvietnam.vn/legal-updates/new-features-of-decree-356-2025-compared-with-decree-13-2023-on-personal-data-protection-892-106712-article.html)

### Cited Findings — TIA dossier
- **Law Article 20(2):** the transfer impact-assessment dossier must be sent to the competent agency (MPS) **within 60 days of the first cross-border transfer**; Article 20(3): assessment done once and updated per Article 22 — [LuatVietnam law text](https://english.luatvietnam.vn/dan-su/law-on-personal-data-protection-law-no-91-2025-qh15-405135-d1.html)
- A05 (Department of Cybersecurity and Hi-Tech Crime Prevention, MPS) has **15 days** from submission to appraise the dossier and may request revisions if incomplete — [Mondaq/Rouse summary](https://rouse.com/insights/news/2026/vietnam-key-developments-in-personal-data-protection-under-decree-no-356-2025-nd-cp-and-issues-businesses-need-to-review)
- Decree 356 "sets out procedures, dossiers, and order for cross-border personal data transfer impact assessments, with **10 templates** attached" — [LuatVietnam feature comparison](https://luatvietnam.vn/legal-updates/new-features-of-decree-356-2025-compared-with-decree-13-2023-on-personal-data-protection-892-106712-article.html)
- **Exemptions — Law Article 20(6):** transfers by competent state agencies; **employee data stored on cloud-computing services**; and data subjects transferring their own data abroad — [LuatVietnam law text](https://english.luatvietnam.vn/dan-su/law-on-personal-data-protection-law-no-91-2025-qh15-405135-d1.html); [Baker McKenzie](https://connectontech.bakermckenzie.com/vietnams-pdpl-in-focus-what-to-know-and-watch-for/)

### Cited Findings — MPS power to halt
- MPS "has the authority to suspend or prohibit transfers that pose threats to national interests," and can act where transfers fail to comply — [Vietnam Briefing](https://www.vietnam-briefing.com/news/vietnam-law-on-personal-data-protection-latest-developments-and-insights.html/)
- National security is a basis to halt cross-border transfers; MPS can investigate, impose administrative penalties, and **prohibit non-compliant cross-border transfers** — [Roedl](https://www.roedl.com/insights/newsflash-asean/2025_03/vietnam-personal-data-protection-law-passed); [Tilleke closer-look series](https://www.tilleke.com/insights/vietnams-new-personal-data-protection-law-a-closer-look/)

### Cited Findings — Consent (Decree 356 operational detail)
- **Decree 356 Article 6:** consent may be in writing or via recorded call, SMS, email, website, app, or other verifiable means; mechanism must verify time, scope, and identity; **default/implied/coerced consent prohibited** (Art. 6.3); burden of proving consent is on the controller (Art. 6.2); sensitive-data subjects must be told the data is sensitive (Art. 6.4) — [LuatVietnam feature comparison](https://luatvietnam.vn/legal-updates/new-features-of-decree-356-2025-compared-with-decree-13-2023-on-personal-data-protection-892-106712-article.html)
- **Law Article 9:** consent must be voluntary, informed, explicit, and granular (separate consent per purpose) and easily revocable; pre-ticked boxes/silence/inactivity invalid; sensitive data needs written/digitally-authenticated consent. **Article 19** covers non-consent processing scenarios; legitimate interest introduced but narrower than GDPR — [Baker McKenzie](https://connectontech.bakermckenzie.com/vietnams-pdpl-in-focus-what-to-know-and-watch-for/); [Vietnam Briefing](https://www.vietnam-briefing.com/news/vietnam-law-on-personal-data-protection-latest-developments-and-insights.html/)

### Cited Findings — Penalties
- **Law Article 8 caps/tiers (amounts to be fixed by separate sanctioning decree):** administrative fines up to **VND 3 billion** (~USD 115,000) for most breaches; **up to 5% of prior-year revenue** for cross-border transfer violations; **up to 10x the illicit gains** for unlawful buying/selling of personal data (VND 3 billion cap if gains incalculable); criminal liability also confirmed — [Baker McKenzie](https://connectontech.bakermckenzie.com/vietnams-pdpl-in-focus-what-to-know-and-watch-for/); [Vietnam Briefing](https://www.vietnam-briefing.com/news/vietnam-law-on-personal-data-protection-latest-developments-and-insights.html/)
- The "5% of revenue OR VND 3 billion, whichever is higher" phrasing for cross-border violations appears in firm summaries — [Roedl / Tilleke](https://www.roedl.com/insights/newsflash-asean/2025_03/vietnam-personal-data-protection-law-passed)

### Cited Findings — Data localization / residency and data-processing services
- No general personal-data localization requirement is created by the PDPL/Decree 356 in the sources reviewed (localization of certain data sits under the Cybersecurity Law and Decree 53/2022/ND-CP, outside this task's scope — flagged, not verified here).
- **De facto residency element — Decree 356 Articles 21 & 22:** providers of **data processing services must be Vietnamese entities** and hold a **data processing services certificate from the MPS** — [LuatVietnam feature comparison](https://luatvietnam.vn/legal-updates/new-features-of-decree-356-2025-compared-with-decree-13-2023-on-personal-data-protection-892-106712-article.html)

### Cited Findings — Breach notification (relevant to SaaS client data)
- **Decree 356 Article 29:** for incidents involving location or biometric data, notify MPS and affected subjects **within 72 hours** of discovery; keep breach records **at least 5 years** after remediation. Banking: Decree 356 Article 8 requires 72-hour notice of leakage/loss of sensitive data — [LuatVietnam feature comparison](https://luatvietnam.vn/legal-updates/new-features-of-decree-356-2025-compared-with-decree-13-2023-on-personal-data-protection-892-106712-article.html)

### Inferences
- For a legal-AI SaaS storing/processing Vietnamese client data on overseas or foreign-provider cloud infrastructure, Article 20(1)(a) and (c) almost certainly make that a regulated cross-border transfer, triggering the TIA dossier to MPS within 60 days — unless a narrow Article 20(6) exemption applies (note: the cloud exemption is limited to *employee* data, not client data, so it likely does NOT cover a SaaS's client-data processing).
- Decree 356 Articles 21–22 (Vietnamese-entity + MPS certificate for data-processing services) are the most consequential structural requirement for a SaaS acting as a processor; confirm against primary text whether a foreign SaaS can qualify or must localize/partner.
