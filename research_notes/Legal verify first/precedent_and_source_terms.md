# Vietnam Án lệ System & Official Legal-Data Source Reuse Terms (verification as of 2026-11-05)

> Scope: (1) the án lệ (precedent) governing instruments + current count; (2) reuse terms of the official legal-document data sources (vbpl.vn / Decree 52/2015, congbobanan.toaan.gov.vn / Resolution 03/2017).
> Note on primary sources: The Supreme Court precedent portal `anle.toaan.gov.vn` serves a valid page but presents an **incomplete TLS certificate chain**, so browser-equivalent fetch tools (WebFetch) reject it. It was reachable via a direct HTTP client and its live HTML was read for the count below. thuvienphapluat.vn and luatminhkhue.vn return HTTP 403 to automated fetches; their content is used only via search snippets, flagged as secondary.

## PART 1 — Án lệ (precedent) governing instruments & current count

### Takeaway
VERIFIED: The precedent system was established by Resolution 03/2015/NQ-HĐTP, is now governed by Resolution 04/2019/NQ-HĐTP (effective 15 July 2019), which was **amended (not replaced)** by Resolution 02/2025/NQ-HĐTP (effective 1 July 2025). "Resolution 04/2025/NQ-HĐTP" is an **unrelated criminal-law instrument** (mitigating/aggravating circumstances), not about precedent. The apply-or-explain duty is confirmed. The **current total is 90 án lệ**, the latest being Án lệ số 90/2026/AL — read directly off the official portal; the "82 vs 90" conflict is a timing artifact (82 at end-2025, 90 after the May 2026 batch).

### Cited Findings

**Governing instruments / chain of authority**
- Resolution **03/2015/NQ-HĐTP** established and first regulated án lệ; it took effect **16/12/2015** and is the original precedent framework — [tapchitoaan.vn research article](https://tapchitoaan.vn/mot-so-van-de-ly-luan-ve-ap-dung-va-phat-trien-an-le11567.html) (via search); [thuvienphapluat statistic "since 03/2015 effective 16/12/2015"](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/banan/tin-tuc/23-ban-an-quyet-dinh-duoc-de-xuat-phat-trien-thanh-an-le-13349.html).
- Resolution **04/2019/NQ-HĐTP** ("quy trình lựa chọn, công bố và áp dụng án lệ") is the **current governing instrument**; issued 18/6/2019, **effective 15/7/2019**, replacing 03/2015 — [luatminhkhue summary of NQ 04/2019](https://luatminhkhue.vn/quy-trinh-lua-chon-cong-bo-va-ap-dung-an-le-theo-nghi-quyet-04-2019-nq-hdtp.aspx) (via search); effective-date 15/7/2019 corroborated across [lsvn.vn](https://lsvn.vn/quy-trinh-lua-chon-cong-bo-va-ap-dung-an-le-a161217.html).
- Resolution **02/2025/NQ-HĐTP** **amended/supplemented** 04/2019 (it did NOT replace it) — it amended **điểm c khoản 2 Điều 6** of NQ 04/2019 (expanding who may propose precedents, e.g. the provincial-court Judges' Committee / Ủy ban Thẩm phán TAND cấp tỉnh). Passed **27/6/2025**, **effective 1/7/2025**. It is a single resolution amending **5** HĐTP resolutions (only part touches precedent) — [plo.vn: "1 nghị quyết sửa đổi, bổ sung 5 nghị quyết"](https://plo.vn/tand-toi-cao-ban-hanh-1-nghi-quyet-sua-doi-bo-sung-5-nghi-quyet-post859146.html); [lsvn.vn on provincial Judges' Committee proposal power](https://lsvn.vn/an-le-duoc-xem-xet-thong-qua-khi-uy-ban-tham-phan-toa-an-nhan-dan-cap-tinh-de-xuat-a160022.html); official full text (.doc) hosted on [vbpl.vn](https://vbpl.vn/TW/Lists/vbpq/Attachments/180155/02_2025_NQ-HDTP_663113.doc).
- After the 02/2025 amendment, NQ 04/2019 is consolidated as **Văn bản hợp nhất số 11/VBHN-TANDTC** (keeps 04/2019 intact with the 02/2025 additions) — [tapchitoaan / search finding](https://tapchitoaan.vn/mot-so-van-de-ly-luan-ve-ap-dung-va-phat-trien-an-le11567.html).

**"Resolution 04/2025/NQ-HĐTP" is NOT about precedent (confirmed)**
- Resolution **04/2025/NQ-HĐTP** is a **criminal-law** instrument guiding application of **mitigating and aggravating circumstances of criminal liability** (Điều 51 & Điều 52 Bộ luật Hình sự). Issued **30/9/2025**, **effective 15/11/2025**, replacing Resolution 01/2000/NQ-HĐTP — [thuvienphapluat full-text record](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Trach-nhiem-hinh-su/Nghi-quyet-04-2025-NQ-HDTP-huong-dan-ap-dung-cac-tinh-tiet-giam-nhe-trach-nhiem-hinh-su-679023.aspx); [lsvn.vn commentary](https://lsvn.vn/nghi-quyet-04-2025-nq-hdtp-buoc-tien-trong-bao-dam-thong-nhat-ap-dung-tinh-tiet-trach-nhiem-hinh-su-a165482.html); [tapchitoaan.vn](https://tapchitoaan.vn/da-co-nghi-quyet-huong-dan-ap-dung-cac-tinh-tiet-giam-nhe-trach-nhiem-hinh-su-cac-tinh-tiet-tang-nang-trach-nhiem-hinh-su14355.html). → Confirms the claim: there is NO "Resolution 04/2025/NQ-HĐTP" governing precedent.

**Apply-or-explain duty**
- VERIFIED (substance): When a case has a legal situation similar to a published precedent, the Judge and People's Assessors **must study and apply the án lệ**, ensuring like cases are decided alike; **if the court does not apply the precedent it must state the reasons clearly in the judgment/decision** — this is Điều 8 ("Áp dụng án lệ trong xét xử") of NQ 04/2019. Corroborated by multiple sources: [thuvienphapluat "Án lệ là gì / khi nào có hiệu lực"](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/banan/tin-tuc/an-le-la-gi-khi-nao-an-le-co-hieu-luc-39.html) and [tapchitoaan practice article](https://tapchitoaan.vn/thuc-trang-ap-dung-an-le-trong-giai-quyet%C2%A0cac-tranh-chap-dan-su-tai-viet-nam11601.html) (both via search). Wording consistency note: the same "must apply / must state reasons if not" rule carried over from Điều 9 of the old NQ 03/2015 into Điều 8 of NQ 04/2019; a precedent also becomes applicable only 30 days after publication (Điều 8).
- Supporting practitioner view (secondary, non-binding): failing to apply an on-point precedent without stated reasons can be a ground for appeal (treated as incorrect application of law) — [luatlongphan.vn](https://lsvn.vn/quy-trinh-lua-chon-cong-bo-va-ap-dung-an-le-a161217.html) / [luatlongphan appeal article](https://luatlongphan.vn/khang-cao-khi-toa-an-khong-ap-dung-an-le).

**Current count (read directly from the official portal)**
- VERIFIED via **anle.toaan.gov.vn** live HTML (portal page `/webcenter/portal/anle/anle`, fetched 2026-11-05): the published án lệ run sequentially; the most recent entries shown are 81/2025/AL, 82/2025/AL, 83/2026/AL … up to **90/2026/AL** (the highest number present). Án lệ are numbered sequentially, so **total published = 90**.
- Corroborated by [thuvienphapluat consolidated list titled "TỔNG HỢP 90 án lệ đã được công bố ở Việt Nam … cập nhật mới nhất 2026"](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/banan/tin-tuc/tong-hop-72-an-le-da-duoc-cong-bo-o-viet-nam-6712) and [lsvn "thêm 8 án lệ mới áp dụng từ 01/7/2026"](https://lsvn.vn/them-8-an-le-moi-ap-dung-tu-ngay-01-7-2026-duoc-toa-an-nhan-dan-toi-cao-cong-bo-a174150.html).
- Latest batch: **Quyết định số 162/QĐ-CA ngày 29/5/2026** published **8 án lệ** (approved by the HĐTP on 21/5/2026), **án lệ số 83–90/2026/AL**, applied in adjudication **from 1/7/2026**. Án lệ số 90/2026/AL concerns a court's jurisdiction to annul a land-use-right certificate (sổ đỏ) — [tapchitoaan QĐ 162/QĐ-CA](https://tapchitoaan.vn/chanh-an-toa-an-nhan-dan-toi-cao-ban-hanh-quyet-dinh-ve-viec-cong-bo-08-an-le15734.html); [hethongphapluat record of án lệ 90/2026/AL](https://hethongphapluat.com/thu-vien-an-le.html).
- Prior batch explaining the "82": **Quyết định 339a/QĐ-CA ngày 25/12/2025** published **10 án lệ (số 73–82/2025/AL)**, making 82 the end-2025 total — [thuvienphapluat QĐ 339a record](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/hoi-dap-phap-luat/toan-van-quyet-dinh-339aqdca-cong-bo-10-an-le-chi-tiet-nhu-the-nao-138079767.html).

### Inferences
- The "82 vs 90" discrepancy in prior research is NOT a real contradiction: 82 was the cumulative total at end-December 2025 (after QĐ 339a); 90 is the cumulative total after the 29/5/2026 batch (QĐ 162). As of late 2026 the correct figure is **90**, latest number **90/2026/AL**.
- Because án lệ are numbered in a single continuous sequence, "latest án lệ number" and "total count" coincide at 90 (no gaps observed in the portal listing).

### Gaps
- I did not read the verbatim Vietnamese text of Điều 8 from a primary full-text source (thuvienphapluat/luatminhkhue return 403 to fetch tools; the official portal's resolution text was not separately pulled). The apply-or-explain substance is confirmed across multiple secondary sources but the exact statutory wording was not quoted from a primary copy. The authoritative consolidated text is **Văn bản hợp nhất 11/VBHN-TANDTC** (recommend pulling from vbpl.vn for a quotable version).
- No án lệ batch later than QĐ 162/QĐ-CA (29/5/2026) was found as of 2026-11-05; a late-2026 batch, if any, would raise the count above 90 — not detected in sources.

## PART 2 — Official legal-document data sources & reuse terms

### Takeaway
VERIFIED: **vbpl.vn** is the National Database on Legal Documents, governed by **Decree 52/2015/NĐ-CP** (effective 20/7/2015); **congbobanan.toaan.gov.vn** is the court-judgment publication portal, governed by **Resolution 03/2017/NQ-HĐTP** (effective 1/7/2017). PARTIALLY VERIFIED on reuse: the national database grants a free public right to access and use documents (Decree 52 Art. 12), but neither instrument found contains an explicit clause authorizing (or prohibiting) **third-party bulk ingestion/redistribution**; that question is a gap and also turns on the Law on Intellectual Property (see Inferences). CAUTION: a draft decree to **replace Decree 52/2015** was in public consultation in 2025 — confirm current status before relying on Decree 52 as the live instrument.

### Cited Findings

**vbpl.vn + Decree 52/2015/NĐ-CP**
- Decree **52/2015/NĐ-CP** "về Cơ sở dữ liệu quốc gia về pháp luật" (National Database on Legal Documents) — issued **28/5/2015**, **effective 20/7/2015** — [caselaw.vn full-text record](https://caselaw.vn/van-ban-phap-luat/116826-nghi-dinh-so-52-2015-nd-cp-ngay-28-05-2015-cua-chinh-phu-ve-co-so-du-lieu-quoc-gia-ve-phap-luat?banan=true); official text on [vbpl.vn PDF](https://vbpl.vn/FileData/TW/Lists/vbpq/Attachments/67193/VanBanGoc_52.2015.N%C4%90.CP.pdf).
- Reuse/access: Mục 3 of the decree covers "khai thác và sử dụng" (exploitation and use) of documents on the database; **Điều 12** grants agencies, organizations and individuals the right to access/use documents via the Internet, the government network and other lawful means; the Ministry of Justice describes access as **free of charge** ("khai thác miễn phí") — [MoJ-linked introduction to CSDLQG & ND 52](https://www.moj.gov.vn/qt/tintuc/Lists/ChiDaoDieuHanh/Attachments/1660/Chuyen%20de%20ve%20gioi%20thieu%20CSDLQG%20ve%20PL%2C%20ND%20co%20ban%20ND%2052%20va%20KH%20trien%20khai%20ND.doc); [SGGP "Khai thác miễn phí văn bản trên CSDLQG về pháp luật"](https://www.sggp.org.vn/khai-thac-mien-phi-van-ban-tren-co-so-du-lieu-quoc-gia-ve-phap-luat-343545.html); Điều 12 quoted on [hethongphapluat](https://hethongphapluat.com/nghi-dinh-52-2015-nd-cp-ve-co-so-du-lieu-quoc-gia-ve-phap-luat.html/dieu-12).
- Replacement-in-progress: in 2025 MoJ consulted on a **draft decree to replace Decree 52/2015** (reported as 26 articles / 4 chapters, two fewer than ND 52) — [MoJ consultation notice](https://moj.gov.vn/portal/tin-tuc/chi-tiet/lay-y-kien-du-thao-nghi-inh-thay-the-nghi-inh-so-522015n-cp-cua-chinh-phu-ve-co-so-du-lieu-quoc-gia-ve-phap-luat-tai-tinh-an-giang-tk0alq4b24.html); a new-version launch of the portal was reported — [SGGP "Khai trương CSDLQG về pháp luật phiên bản mới"](https://www.sggp.org.vn/khai-truong-co-so-du-lieu-quoc-gia-ve-phap-luat-phien-ban-moi-post849364.html).

**congbobanan.toaan.gov.vn + Resolution 03/2017/NQ-HĐTP**
- Resolution **03/2017/NQ-HĐTP** "về việc công bố bản án, quyết định trên Cổng thông tin điện tử của Tòa án" — effective **1/7/2017** (passage date reported variously as 16/3/2017 or 17/2/2017 across sources; verify against primary). Implemented by Công văn 144/TANDTC-PC (4/7/2017) — [hoatieu full-text record of NQ 03/2017](https://hoatieu.vn/phap-luat/nghi-quyet-03-2017-nq-hdtp-cong-bo-ban-an-quyet-dinh-tren-cong-thong-tin-dien-tu-toa-an-123144); [Công văn 144/TANDTC-PC](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/cong-van/Cong-nghe-thong-tin/Cong-van-144-TANDTC-PC-2017-thi-hanh-Nghi-quyet-03-2017-NQ-HDTP-353676.aspx).
- What it regulates (relevant to reuse/scope): judgments in force that qualify must be posted within **30 days** of taking effect; it defines categories **excluded** from publication (not-yet-effective; closed-session trials; cases involving persons under 18; state secrets / professional secrets / undisclosed financial-investment info) and mandates **anonymization/encoding** of names, addresses and personal/family-secret information before publication — [tapchitoaan analysis](https://tapchitoaan.vn/ban-ve-cong-bo-ban-an-quyet-dinh-tren-cong-thong-tin-dien-tu-toa-an); [thuvienphapluat Q&A on posting deadline](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/hoi-dap-phap-luat/thoi-han-cong-bo-ban-an-tren-cong-thong-tin-dien-tu-cua-toa-an-la-bao-lau-138025546.html).

### Inferences
- Decree 52's framing is about **public right to access and use** the database content for free; it is an access-provisioning instrument, not a licensing/redistribution charter. It does not, on the sources found, expressly grant OR forbid a third party to systematically ingest and redistribute the corpus.
- The decisive legal basis for whether a third party may reuse/redistribute these texts is almost certainly **copyright, not these decrees**: under Vietnam's Law on Intellectual Property, "văn bản quy phạm pháp luật, văn bản hành chính … và bản dịch chính thức của các văn bản đó" are excluded from copyright protection. Court judgments published on the portal are state-produced documents. This strongly suggests the *underlying legal/judgment texts* are not copyright-protected, but this needs confirmation against Điều 15 Luật Sở hữu trí tuệ (NOT verified in this research round — see Gaps). Separately, the portals' own database structure, metadata and compilation, and the anonymization obligations on judgments, are distinct constraints.
- For a legal-AI product: ingesting the *content* of laws/judgments is on firmer ground than copying a portal's curated database wholesale; and judgment reuse must respect the anonymization regime of NQ 03/2017. (Do NOT rely on scraping commercial aggregators such as thuvienphapluat/luatvietnam, whose compilations carry their own ToS.)

### Gaps
- No explicit "terms of use" / bulk-access / API or redistribution clause was located in the text of **Decree 52/2015** or **Resolution 03/2017**, nor a published site ToS for vbpl.vn or congbobanan.toaan.gov.vn. The specific reuse/redistribution permission question is **unresolved** — recommend reading the full articles of ND 52 (esp. Mục 3, Điều 12, and any provision on "sao chép"/"phổ biến") and any site Terms page directly.
- The copyright-exclusion point (Điều 15 Luật SHTT) was not fetched/verified this round; it is load-bearing for the product and should be verified from the primary IP statute.
- Decree 52/2015 **current in-force status** is uncertain given the 2025 replacement draft — confirm whether a successor decree has been promulgated and, if so, its number/effective date and any new reuse terms.
- Resolution 03/2017 exact passage date conflicts across secondary sources (16/3/2017 vs 17/2/2017); effective date 1/7/2017 is consistent. Verify passage date against the primary text.
- The án lệ portal (anle.toaan.gov.vn) could not be read by the standard fetch tool due to an incomplete TLS certificate chain; the count was obtained via a direct HTTP client reading the live page HTML. If strict primary-source browser verification is required, note the TLS-chain caveat.
